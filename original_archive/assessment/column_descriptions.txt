## KEY

IsHCA - Indicator whether team has home court advantage or not
IsConference - Indicator whether match is between two teams in the same conference
2PMade etc - 2 pointer made/missed/attempted
3PMade etc - 3 pointer made/missed/attempted
FTMade etc - Free throw made/missed/attempted
ORB - offensive rebounds
DRB - defensive rebounds
TOV - Turnovers commited by team
PF - Fouls commited by team
